Questions

Before you sign up

Is it free to join?

Yes. Creating an account is free and you don't need a card to sign up. Students pay per lesson; tutors keep 80% of every one-to-one booking.

When am I charged?

Only after your tutor accepts your request. You then pay by card to confirm the lesson. If you don't pay in time, the booking simply expires and nothing is charged.

Who are the tutors?

University students teaching courses they've already taken. Tutors who confirm a university email address get the Verified badge and appear first in search.

How do tutors get paid?

Through Stripe, to your bank account, 48 hours after each completed lesson. You keep 80% of every one-to-one booking.
