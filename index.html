Payment protection

Your money is protected at every step.

Paying someone you haven't met should feel safe. Here's exactly what happens to your payment.

  2. Held for 48 hours

    Your tutor's payout stays on hold until 48 hours after the lesson.

  3. Report a problem

    Tutor didn't show up? Report it within 48 hours. Their payout is paused while we review it, and a no-show is refunded in full.

  4. Tutor cancels?

    You're refunded in full, automatically — whenever they cancel.

Questions

Before you book

Anything else? Write to info@uniskilled.com.

Who are the tutors?

University students teaching courses they've already taken. Tutors who confirm a university email address get the Verified badge and appear first in search.

When am I charged?

Only after your tutor accepts your request. You then pay by card to confirm the lesson. If you don't pay in time, the booking simply expires — nothing is charged.

What if the lesson doesn't happen?

If your tutor cancels, you're refunded in full automatically. If they don't show up, use "Report an Issue" on the session within 48 hours — their payout is paused, we review it, and you get a full refund.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Cancel more than 24 hours before the lesson, or within 30 minutes of paying, for a full refund. Later than that there's no refund, but you can propose a new time instead. Details are in the refund policy.

How much does it cost?

Tutors set their own rates, and you can propose your own price in your request. The minimum booking is €15. Group sessions split the cost between up to 4 students.

How do tutors get paid?

Through Stripe, to your bank account, 48 hours after each completed lesson. You keep 80% of every one-to-one booking.
