What happens if something goes wrong.

Tutor doesn't show up

You're refunded automatically. No form to fill in.

Session wasn't what was agreed

Report it within 48 hours. A person reviews every case and can refund you in full or in part.

Someone asks to pay outside UniSkilled

Don't. Off-platform payments have no refund protection and we can't help with disputes.

Coming later: freelance projects. Businesses will be able to hire verified students for real work, with the same payment protection as tutoring.
