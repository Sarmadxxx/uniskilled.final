What we do

Two things.
Done properly.

Peer tutoring is live now — students teach the courses they know. A freelance marketplace, where students take on projects for businesses, is coming next.

Tutors earn the ✓ Verified badge by confirming a code sent to their university inbox — so you can see at a glance who's been checked. Payments are held by UniSkilled until 48 hours after each session, so there's time to report a problem before the tutor is paid.

🎓 Tutoring
1
Student signs up as a tutor
Any email works to sign up. To earn the Verified badge, they confirm a code sent to their university inbox.
2
They list the subjects they can teach
Set their rate, availability, and format (online, in-person, or both).
3
Students book and pay through the platform
You're only charged once the tutor accepts. Payment is then held until 48 hours after the session.
4
Tutor is paid automatically after the session
Funds are released 48 hours after the session, unless an issue has been reported.
