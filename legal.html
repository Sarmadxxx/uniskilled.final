Refund & Cancellation Policy

EU Statutory Withdrawal Right EU consumers generally have a 14-day right to withdraw from distance contracts. Tutoring sessions are booked for a specific date and time, similar to a restaurant reservation or hotel booking, and this Refund & Cancellation Policy is offered as UniSkilled's own voluntary cancellation terms — intentionally more generous than the statutory minimum in most cases.

Cancellation by the Student

  • Within 30 minutes of paying: 100% refund, regardless of how soon the session is — this covers accidental bookings or an immediate change of mind.
  • More than 24 hours before the session: 100% refund.
  • Less than 24 hours before the session (outside the 30-minute window above): No refund, but the student can reschedule the session to a new date/time at no extra cost instead of losing the payment.
  • No-show, without cancelling: No refund.
  • Student did not enjoy the lesson: No refund. See "Dissatisfaction with a Session" below.

Cancellation by the Tutor

  • Tutor cancels, at any time: Student receives a 100% refund, automatically, with no time restriction.
  • Tutor doesn't show up: Student receives a 100% refund once they report it using "Report an Issue" on the session within 48 hours of the session's scheduled end time. The tutor's payout is paused as soon as the report is made, and UniSkilled reviews it before issuing the refund.
  • Repeated last-minute cancellations or no-shows by tutors may result in account suspension.

Tutor Payout Eligibility

  • After a session is marked complete, payment is held for 48 hours before being released to the tutor.
  • If the session is cancelled before that release (by either party, under the terms above), no payout occurs and any refund due is issued automatically.

Refund Process

Refunds under this policy are issued automatically and instantly by using the "Cancel" option on your session in your UniSkilled dashboard — no need to email support for a standard cancellation. Refunds are returned to the card or other payment method used at checkout, and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer. For any cancellation outside these standard cases, or a payment issue that the automatic system doesn't resolve, contact info@uniskilled.com with your booking reference.

If a valid refund is approved after a tutor's payout has already been released, UniSkilled reserves the right to recover the refunded amount from the tutor's future earnings or to otherwise seek reimbursement where permitted by law.

Dissatisfaction with a Session

Tutoring outcomes are subjective and depend on many factors, including the student's preparation and participation. A refund will not be issued solely because a student was dissatisfied with the teaching style or expected a different outcome.

Refunds may only be considered where:

  • the tutor failed to attend the session;
  • the tutor ended the session significantly earlier than agreed without justification;
  • the session could not reasonably be delivered due to technical issues caused by the tutor; or
  • the session was materially different from what was advertised on the tutor's profile.

Students must report any issue within 48 hours of the session being marked complete, using the "Report an Issue" option on the session in your UniSkilled dashboard (or by contacting info@uniskilled.com if you're unable to access your account). This window matches the 48-hour period during which the tutor's payout is held, described under "Payment Processing" below. UniSkilled may request supporting information before making a decision. No dispute raised after this 48-hour window will be considered.

Payment Processing

All payments for tutoring sessions are processed securely by our payment provider, Stripe — by card or by the other methods shown at checkout (such as Apple Pay, Google Pay or Klarna). Students are charged only after the tutor has accepted the booking request. The payment is held and is not paid out to the tutor until the session has been completed and the 48-hour hold has passed.

Tutor earnings become available 48 hours after the session is marked complete. UniSkilled reviews any issue reported to info@uniskilled.com within that window before the payout is released.

Once released, payouts are sent automatically to the tutor's chosen payout method. UniSkilled's 20% platform fee is deducted before the tutor's payout is released. A detailed breakdown of the payment, fee, and payout amount is available in the tutor's account.

Payment Terms

Tutor Payouts

Where UniSkilled processes payments, funds are held until the tutoring session has been completed and the 48-hour hold period below has passed.

  • Tutor earnings become available 48 hours after the scheduled session end time, and are then paid out automatically to the bank account the tutor connected through Stripe.
  • To receive payouts, a tutor must connect a Stripe payout account and complete Stripe's identity verification before accepting bookings. Payouts are currently available to tutors whose bank account is in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States or Canada.
  • UniSkilled deducts its 20% platform fee before releasing the payout, so the tutor receives 80% of the session price. A full breakdown of the session price, platform fee, and net payout is displayed in the tutor's account.

Session Fees

UniSkilled enforces a minimum booking value of €10 (or the equivalent in the tutor's listed currency) per session. Tutors set their own hourly rate, and the final price is calculated from the rate and the session duration the student selects; a booking cannot be confirmed below this minimum.

Tutors set their own hourly rates, displayed in their chosen currency on their profile. The price shown when booking is the total the student pays — UniSkilled does not add a booking or service fee for students. UniSkilled's platform fee is taken from the tutor's side (see "Platform Fee" below).

Payment Methods

All sessions booked on UniSkilled are paid through UniSkilled's checkout, processed by Stripe (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna or other methods shown at checkout). Students pay only after the tutor has accepted the booking request, and must complete payment within 24 hours of acceptance or the booking expires and the slot is released. The payment is held until the session is complete and the 48-hour hold has passed, then the tutor's payout is released.

Tutors and students must not arrange or accept payment for sessions booked through UniSkilled by any other means (such as bank transfer, cash, cryptocurrency, or a direct PayPal payment between users). Payments made outside UniSkilled are not covered by the 48-hour hold, dispute resolution, or refund protections, and may breach the Non-Circumvention Policy.

Referral & Account Credit Terms

Referral Program

UniSkilled operates an optional referral program that allows users to invite others to the platform in exchange for account credit. Participation is voluntary. This section describes how it works and the terms that apply.

How It Works

  • Every user has a unique referral link, available from their dashboard.
  • A new user who signs up using that link receives €10 welcome credit (subject to change), which is used on their tutoring bookings as described under "Using Account Credit" below.
  • The person who referred them receives €10 account credit (subject to change) once the new user's first paid session qualifies, as described below.

Using Account Credit

  • Credit is applied automatically at checkout when booking a one-to-one tutoring session. On each booking it reduces the price by up to UniSkilled's platform fee for that booking (currently 20% of the session price). Any credit not used on a booking stays in the user's account for later bookings.
  • The discount, the amount charged, and any credit kept for later are shown at checkout before payment.
  • Credit is currently not available on group sessions.
  • Account credit has no cash value. It cannot be paid out, withdrawn, transferred to another user, or exchanged for money, and it can only be used toward tutoring sessions booked on UniSkilled. Tutors can use credit when they book sessions as a student; credit is never added to a tutor's payout.
  • If a booking paid partly with credit is fully refunded, the money paid is refunded to the original payment method and the credit used is returned to the user's account.
  • Credit does not currently expire. If UniSkilled introduces an expiry date, users will be notified at least 30 days in advance, and the change will not apply retroactively to shorten the time already given.

When a Referral Qualifies

A referral only qualifies, and credit is only issued, once the invited user's first paid session has been marked complete, has passed the same 48-hour hold period described under "Refund & Cancellation Policy" and "Payment Processing" above, and no dispute was opened against it within that window. A signup or a booking alone does not qualify a referral — the session must genuinely go through undisputed. If a qualifying session is later refunded or found to be fraudulent, UniSkilled reserves the right to reverse any credit already issued as a result of it.

Discounts Funded by UniSkilled, Not Tutors

Welcome credit and account credit are funded by UniSkilled out of its platform fee. They never reduce the amount a tutor is paid — tutors always receive their normal 80% share of the full session price, calculated as if no credit had been used.

Fraud Prevention

Referring yourself, creating duplicate or fake accounts, or otherwise attempting to manipulate the referral program is a violation of these Terms. UniSkilled may void any referral, reverse any credit, and suspend the accounts involved where fraudulent or abusive activity is identified.

Changes to the Program

UniSkilled may modify, suspend, or discontinue the referral program, its reward amounts, or its eligibility conditions at any time. Credit already earned under the terms in effect at the time it was issued will not be retroactively cancelled as a result of such a change, except where the Fraud Prevention clause above applies.

This isn't only about fees. A session booked and paid for outside UniSkilled has none of the protections described in this policy — no 48-hour payment hold, no dispute resolution, no refund guarantee, and no record toward a tutor's verified track record. We built this policy to protect both sides, not just to collect a commission.
